# Obsidian notes

Read existing notes and templates first. Do not invent wikilinks, overwrite existing notes, or create multiple notes without confirming the proposed tree when the split is substantial. Preserve the vault's frontmatter and heading conventions, use Spanish by default, and distinguish suggestions from edits.
